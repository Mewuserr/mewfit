# Design System: MewFit

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Forge"**

MewFit is a premium sci-fi game of its own. The page is near-black space (void) with a slow nebula: a magenta bloom off the top right, a cyan one on the left and a violet one rising from the bottom, fixed to the viewport, with a canvas of small glowing motes drifting upward behind everything. The game lives on mission plates: panels with two chamfered corners (top-left and bottom-right), framed in an iridescent metal that runs cyan through violet and a dark middle to magenta and gold, and lit from inside by a violet glow at the top. A slow band of light sweeps across each plate. The player card adds a moving holographic foil. The brand colour is the four-stop holo gradient (cyan, violet, magenta, gold): it fills the primary actions, the earned title tag, the train diamond in the nav and the crest ring, and is clipped into the "Fit" of the wordmark.

Standing is a diamond rank crest. A rim tinted by the rank colour holds the rank letter, ringed by a thin holo ring and a blurred rank-coloured aura, both turning slowly. Progress reads as segmented HUD bars: EXP in gold, ENERGY (today's missions) in blue-violet-magenta. The five stats are a pentagon radar. Quests and tools are illustrated item icons, small gradient-shaded objects (a gold dumbbell, a water drop, a drumstick, a scroll) set in octagonal frames that glow in the quest's colour. The system speaks through a mission announcement panel at the top of Today. It holds a spinning sigil, a typed-out line and a listen button, and it replaced the earlier talking cat. This is the owner's chosen direction. It is not Solo Leveling and uses none of that IP's names or text. Every label a user reads is MewFit's own Thai.

The app is dark only (`color-scheme: dark`, `theme-color` and manifest colours void). The layout is built for phones first (390px design width, safe down to 360px), with 16px gutters, a 14px rhythm between blocks and touch targets of 44px or more. Compact chips and inline quest controls are 34px.

**Identity asset.** The logo and every app icon (`icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`, `icon-maskable-512.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, and `icon.svg`, which wraps the PNG in a 15/64-radius clip) show a kettlebell whose handle forms a cat's ears, with eyes, nose and whiskers cut out as negative space. The shape was first generated with ChatGPT image generation on 2026-10-29 at the owner's request, replacing a leaping-cat mark the owner rejected as too close to Puma. That first mark was off-white on orange-red. For the arcade skin the same shape was rebuilt locally from the ChatGPT mark's outline and re-coloured: the kettlebell-cat is filled with a cyan-violet-pink gradient, sits on a deep indigo field and has a soft glow. It was then exported locally to every size. The Forge skin keeps these files unchanged. The logo shows as the 34px mark in the top bar and the 72px mark in the onboarding hero, both with a 10px radius. The wordmark "Mew**Fit**" is set in Chakra Petch, with "Fit" clipped to the holo gradient.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Void space with a three-colour nebula and rising light motes.
- Chamfered mission plates in an iridescent metal frame; the player plate carries holo foil.
- One holo gradient (cyan, violet, magenta, gold) for primary actions, the title tag, the train diamond, the crest ring and the wordmark's "Fit".
- A diamond rank crest with turning rings; segmented HUD bars; a pentagon stat radar.
- Illustrated item icons in octagonal frames, one colour per quest.
- Gold is reward: XP, the countdown, CLEAR stamps, the reward strip and LEVEL UP.
- Chakra Petch for the system (headings, numbers, HUD); Sarabun, a looped Thai face, for every sentence.

## Colors

Void-black space lit by four iridescent colours that together form the holo, plus quest colours that each own a mission.

### Primary
- **Holo** (forge-cyan, 35% forge-violet, 65% forge-magenta, forge-gold, at 120 degrees): the brand. It fills primary and hot buttons, the earned title tag, the raised train diamond, the move-number squares in the move strip and completed week days, and is the outer ring of the crest. It is clipped into text for exactly two things: "Fit" in the wordmark and earned title names. Text on it is void (6.2:1 or better on every stop).
- **Frame** (a 135-degree run of forge-cyan 95%, forge-violet 45% at 28%, white 7% at 50%, forge-magenta 50% at 74%, forge-gold 95%): the iridescent metal of mission plates and modals. It is a frame, never a fill.
- **Forge Cyan** (forge-cyan): also the interface accent (`--accent`, `--go`): links, focus rings, the caret, text selection, selected chips and segments, checked options, default progress bars, the onboarding progress, today in the week strip, active nav glow, the mission panel's left rule and typing cursor, and the stat codes on the Progress view.

### Secondary: quest colours
Each daily quest owns one colour. It tints that row (9%, 18% when done, with a 45% rim), fills its item frame and its progress bar, and colours the frame's glow.
- **Forge Gold** (forge-gold): the main training mission. Gold is also the reward colour: every "+n XP", the midnight countdown (with a gold glow), the CLEAR stamp, the reward strip and title tiles (30% rim on 5%), the EXP bar and the LEVEL UP glow.
- **Quest Teal** (quest-teal): the recovery quest on rest days.
- **Forge Cyan**: the water quest.
- **Quest Orange** (quest-orange): the protein quest.
- **Forge Green** (forge-green): the steps quest. It is also `--good`: done sets, the done badge on item frames, food dots, water glasses and verdicts.
- **Forge Violet** (forge-violet): the sleep quest. It also tints training days in the week strip (14%) and is the middle of the radar.

### Tertiary
- **Forge Magenta** (forge-magenta): `--hot`. The ENERGY label and bar end, figure props, the onboarding feature numbers, `hot` chips, the sleep dot on the timeline and the radar's vertex glow. It is never a quest colour.
- **Accent Soft** (accent-soft) / **Hot Soft** (hot-soft): tinted fills for banners, OK notes, list hover and selected options (accent soft), and notes, `hot` chips and feature tiles (hot soft).

### Rank ladder
- **E** (rank-e, steel), **D** (rank-d, green), **C** (rank-c, sky), **B** (rank-b, violet), **A** (rank-a, orange), **S** (rank-s, gold). The current rank colour is passed to the crest as `--rk`. It tints the diamond rim, the letter and the crest's glow and aura.

### Neutral
- **Void** (void): the page ground, the safe-area strip, the rope surfaces (rest timer, HIIT overlay), the inside of HUD bars and text on every holo or bright fill.
- **Plate Top / Plate Bottom** (plate-top to plate-bottom at 85%): the lit interior of mission plates, under a 22% violet bloom at the top. **Modal Top** (modal-top) to plate-bottom: the modal interior.
- **Card Top / Card Bottom**: the fill of ordinary cards, tips, tool tiles and the hero, inside a hairline frame of cyan 35%, white 6% and magenta 30%. The nav bar uses a near-opaque version of the same fill.
- **Top Glass** (top-glass): the sticky top bar. **Field** (field) with **Field Line** (field-line): every input. **Hairline** (hairline): the nav rim, rest bar, KPI rules and move chips. **Wash** (wash): week days and the default button's lower stop.
- **Surface** (surface) and **Sunk** (sunk): base-layer fills still used by desktop tabs, segments, option cards, chips, target tiles and bar tracks.
- **Ink** (ink): text and figure strokes. **White** (white): the level number, radar values, the active nav label and LEVEL UP. **Muted** (muted): secondary text, nav labels, captions, counts and HUD values. **Line** (line): hairlines between list rows and table cells.
- **Rope Line / Rope Track / Prop**: outlines and props on rope surfaces, the HIIT track, and stick-figure props.

### Semantic
- **Good** is forge-green and **Warn** is forge-gold. **Bad** (bad): the danger button, the "ความเชื่อ:" myth prefix and the streak warning.

### Named Rules
**The Holo Is Earned Rule.** The holo gradient means MewFit itself, the main thing to do next or something earned: the wordmark's "Fit", primary actions, the train diamond, the crest ring, the earned title tag and completed days. Ordinary cards get only the faint hairline frame.

**The One Colour Per Quest Rule.** Each quest owns one colour (training gold, recovery teal, water cyan, protein orange, steps green, sleep violet) and its row tint, item frame, bar and done rim all use it. Done state is also shown by the green check badge and the muted quest text, never by colour alone.

**The Gold Is Reward Rule.** Gold marks what you earn: XP, the bonus, the countdown to midnight, CLEAR, titles and LEVEL UP. Every "+n XP" is gold whatever the quest's colour.

**The Rank Lights The Crest Rule.** The rank colour appears only in the crest: its rim, letter, glow and aura. The outer ring is always holo.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Chakra Petch (with Kanit, Leelawadee UI, Noto Sans Thai, system-ui), loaded at 500-700.
**Body Font:** Sarabun (with Leelawadee UI, Noto Sans Thai Looped, Noto Sans Thai, system-ui). It was chosen in a readability pass after the owner said the text was hard to read.
**Numeral Font:** Chakra Petch with tabular figures.
**Data Font:** the system monospace stack, used only in the backup/restore JSON textarea (0.8rem) and the recovery code (1.25rem, 0.06em tracking).

**Character:** Chakra Petch is squared and technical and covers Thai, so headings, numbers and HUD labels read like a game interface. Sarabun's looped Thai forms keep the coaching copy calm and easy to read.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (700, clamp(2rem, 8.5vw, 2.8rem), 1.12): the session name in the hero.
- **Headline** (700, clamp(1.8rem, 7.4vw, 2.5rem), 1.2): the page `h1`, with a muted Sarabun 0.93rem line under it. Onboarding uses clamp(1.9rem, 7vw, 2.6rem) with -0.02em tracking.
- **Level Up** (700, 2.9rem, 0.08em): "LEVEL UP!" in white with a gold and magenta glow.
- **Crest Letter**: the rank letter, drawn in the crest SVG at 44/120 of the crest (about 43px, 55px in the big crest) with a white-to-rank gradient fill.
- **Player Name** (700, 1.55rem, 1.1) and **Level Number** (700, 1.9rem, white) after a muted "Lv.", followed by the muted rank name.
- **Title** (700, 1.25rem): the Thai title of every plate and card `h2`. Player move names scale to clamp(1.4rem, 5vw, 1.9rem) and the HIIT move name to clamp(1.4rem, 5vw, 2.2rem). **Title Small** (600, 1.05rem): `h3`, option titles and buttons.
- **Body** (400, 16.5px, 1.7): all reading text, capped at 52-62ch. **Quest Text** is body at 600 with 1.35 line height. Small text is 0.93rem.
- **Label** (Sarabun 500-600, 0.86-0.92rem): chips, small buttons, tile captions, table heads and field labels. **Nav Label** (0.8rem) is the one Thai exception below 0.86rem.
- **HUD Label** (Chakra Petch 700, 0.8rem, 0.14em): the Latin words EXP and ENERGY, in the bar's colour. **HUD Value** (Chakra Petch 0.86rem, muted): bar values, quest counts, plate sub-labels and "+n XP" (0.84rem, 700, gold). Thai has no case and nothing is uppercased by CSS.
- **Numerals** (Chakra Petch, tabular): metrics at 1.05rem 600, KPIs as a ruled row at 2.1rem, the rest bar countdown at 1.5rem. Live numerals step up by scale: the rep stepper is 3.2rem, the rest timer 4.2rem and the HIIT clock clamp(4rem, 20vw, 8rem). Chakra Petch tops out at 700, so the 800 weights declared in the CSS render at 700.

### Named Rules
**The Two Voices Rule.** Chakra Petch is the system (headings, numbers, HUD labels, buttons). Sarabun is the coach (sentences, quest text, announcements). Never set a paragraph in Chakra Petch.

**The Live Number Rule.** The number the user is acting on right now is the largest thing on screen (3.2rem or more), tabular and in ink. Colour isn't used to make it louder.

**The Two Holo Words Rule.** Holo-clipped text is used for "Fit" in the wordmark and earned title names. Everything else that reads is set in solid colour.

## Layout

The page is one centred column, 1000px wide at most, with 16px gutters that respect `env(safe-area-inset-*)`. Each view is a vertical stack with a 14px gap. Plates have 20px × 18px padding and a 14px inner gap. Cards have 18px padding. Cards stack singly on phones and form 2- or 3-up grids from 740px. KPI stats auto-fit at a 130px minimum, form fields at 140px, and titles at 150px.

Today opens with three plates in order: the player plate (crest, identity, HUD bars, compact radar), the mission announcement, then the daily mission plate (coach line, move strip, quest list, reward strip, start button). Ordinary cards follow (week, tools, tip). The player plate puts the 118px crest beside the identity column (20px gap), with two HUD rows below. Each HUD row is a 62px / 1fr / auto grid. The radar is at most 300px wide, or 210px in its compact form on Today. Quest rows are a 48px / 1fr / auto grid with a 12px gap, stacked 8px apart, with the "+n XP" over the row's control in the right column.

Navigation is a floating bar fixed 10px above the bottom safe area. It sits 12px from the sides, is at most 520px wide and has five columns. Main content reserves 116px of bottom padding for it. The bar is hidden during a workout and during onboarding. At 880px it gives way to pill tabs in the top bar. On wide screens the workout player puts the figure in a sticky left column.

Breakpoints: 420px (tool grid), 640px (hero and option grids), 740px (card grids), 760px (player split), 880px (desktop nav).

## Elevation & Depth

Depth comes from lit metal over space. The nebula and motes light the field. Plates are not shadowed: the clip-path cuts them out sharply and their frame and inner violet bloom make them glow. Ordinary cards sit on a deep drop with a 7% top highlight. Coloured light is kept for things that are alive or earned: the crest glows in its rank colour, item icons glow in their quest colour, HUD bars carry a glowing tip, quest bars glow, the countdown and CLEAR glow gold, the radar and the mission sigil glow, and LEVEL UP burns gold. The modal scrim is void at 80%.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Card drop** (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,.07), 0 24px 50px -26px rgba(0,0,0,.95)`): every card, tip, tool tile and the hero.
- **Nav drop** (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,.12), 0 18px 40px -16px rgba(0,0,0,.95)`): the floating nav bar.
- **Crest glow** (`filter: drop-shadow(0 0 12px` rank colour at 70%`) drop-shadow(0 8px 14px rgba(0,0,0,.7))`), plus the blurred aura ring: the rank crest.
- **Item glow** (`filter: drop-shadow(0 2px 3px rgba(0,0,0,.7)) drop-shadow(0 0 6px` quest colour at 60%`)`): item icons in their frames. Tool icons use a violet 50% glow at 8px, the active nav icon a cyan 70% glow at 8px.
- **Bar tip** (`box-shadow: 0 0 10px 3px var(--hc)`): the 3px white tip of a HUD bar. Quest bars glow `0 0 8px` of the quest colour.
- **Reward glow** (`text-shadow: 0 0 10px rgba(255,200,87,.6)`): the countdown. CLEAR uses 12px at 90%. LEVEL UP uses `0 0 10px gold, 0 0 28px gold, 0 0 50px rgba(255,79,216,.5)`.
- **Field focus** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(61,224,255,.18)`): focused inputs.

### Named Rules
**The Light Means Live Rule.** Coloured light appears only on the crest, item icons, bar fills, the radar, the sigil, rewards and LEVEL UP. Body text, list rows and secondary controls never glow.

## Shapes

Two families. The **game layer is chamfered**: mission plates cut 18px at the top-left and bottom-right corners; holo primary buttons cut 10px (6px when small) at the same corners; item frames are octagons with 8px cuts; the title tag and every HUD and quest bar are parallelograms slanted 8px, 6px and 3px; the train button is a diamond; the crest is a diamond inside rings. **Everything else is softly rounded**: cards, tips, tools and the hero 12px; default buttons and inputs 8px; quest rows, the reward strip, title tiles and the toast 10px; week days and move chips 8px with 4px number squares; chips 6px; the nav bar and modals 16px. Circles are used for the crest rings, legend dots and spark particles. The logo tile is 10px.

Some controls inherited from earlier builds keep their own radii: option cards 18px, banners and figure boxes 16px, notes and target tiles 14px, set dots 12px, the rest timer 22px, and segments, desktop tabs and plain progress tracks as full pills.

### Named Rules
**The Chamfer Means Game Rule.** A cut corner says "this is the game": a mission, the primary action, an item, a bar, a title, rank. Information and tools sit in rounded cards.

## Components

### Mission Plate (signature)
The player plate, the announcement, the daily mission plate and the titles plate.
- **Frame:** the iridescent frame gradient fills the clipped shape and shows as a 1.5px edge around the interior (plate-top to plate-bottom under a 22% violet bloom). Chamfer 18px.
- **Header:** the Thai title in Title, with a muted HUD-value sub-label at the right (streak, time left, titles earned). A 1px rule underneath fades from cyan through violet 40% to nothing.
- **Light:** a 7% white band sweeps across every 7s. The player plate adds holo foil (cyan, magenta and gold at 8-10%, screen blend) drifting over 9s.
- **Motion:** as a view opens, plates rise 14px from 2× brightness over 0.7s (cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)); the next plate follows 80ms later.

### Rank Crest (signature)
- **Form:** 118px (150px in the level-up window). An SVG diamond: a rim graded white, rank colour, violet, magenta; an inner diamond lit from the top (#232A55 to void); a faint inset outline and chevron; the letter; a small muted "RANK" beneath.
- **Rings:** a 3px holo conic ring 8px outside the crest turns every 8s. A blurred aura ring 16px outside, mixing the rank colour, magenta and cyan at 55%, turns the other way every 12s and pulses over 3s. The diamond floats 3px over 4s.
- `role="img"` with an `aria-label` naming the rank.

### HUD Bars
EXP (gold ramp #B7791F → gold → #FFF1C4) and ENERGY (#1E9BFF → violet → magenta, quests done today). Each is 14px, a slanted parallelogram over a sunken void track with ten segment ticks. The fill has a top gloss, a glowing 3px white tip and a shimmer every 2.6s, and grows over 1.2s. Label in HUD Label and the bar colour, value in HUD Value. Bars are `role="progressbar"` with values.

### Stat Radar
A pentagon of STR, AGI, VIT, END and INT: four 9% white grid rings and 7% axes, an area filled cyan 35% to magenta 30% with a cyan-violet-magenta 2.2px stroke and violet glow, white vertex dots glowing magenta. The area breathes over 3s. Latin codes are muted, values white 700. The Progress view adds a stat list: a cyan code over a muted Thai explanation.

### Item Icons (signature)
Illustrated objects, not line icons: each is a 48-unit SVG of gradient-shaded shapes with a light rim and a white highlight, drawn from one registry (train, recover, water, protein, steps, sleep, bolt, scale, scroll, calendar, crest).
- **In a frame** (quests): a 48px octagon filled with the quest colour at 34% fading to void, a 1.5px inner rim of the quest colour at 75% and a top gloss. The icon is 34px. A done quest adds a 16px green check badge at the lower right.
- **Bare** (tools, nav): 48px in tool tiles with a violet glow; 30px in the nav.

### Daily Missions
- **Row:** a quest-colour tint over a 2% wash, 6% white rim, 10px radius. The item frame leads. Next comes the quest text with, for counted quests, a 6px slanted bar in the quest colour and a "v/max" count. The right column holds the gold "+n XP" over the control.
- **Logged in place:** water has a − button and a +1 holo button; steps and sleep take 82px number inputs (16px text); protein links to the Eat view with "บันทึก"; recovery has "เริ่ม" until done. Inline controls are 34px.
- **Done:** the tint rises to 18% with a 45% rim, the check badge appears, the text turns muted and "+n XP" drops to 50%. A newly done quest flashes gold and a gold "CLEAR" stamp lands at an -8 degree angle and fades over 1.6s.
- **Reward strip:** gold 30% rim on 5% gold, muted text with the gold "+100 XP". When everything is done the rim turns full gold and the text ink. A muted streak warning sits under it until then.
- The plate also carries the coach's line (muted), the move strip (8px chips with holo number squares) and the start button with a default "ดูท่าในภารกิจ" button beside or below it.

### Mission Announcement
A plate that replaced the talking cat. Its header holds a 46px sigil (a holo diamond and dashed ring whose outer points turn every 6s, with a cyan glow), the Thai title "ประกาศภารกิจ" and a small "ฟัง" button. Below, a tappable panel with a 2px cyan left rule over a cyan 8% fade shows one line at a time in Sarabun 500, typed out grapheme by grapheme with a blinking cyan cursor, and a muted "next" hint. "ฟัง" reads the line aloud in Thai at a lowered pitch, and the plate brightens while it speaks. Under reduced motion the line appears whole.

### Titles
Auto-fill tiles at least 150px wide: gold 30% rim on 5% gold, 10px radius. Earned names are holo-clipped Chakra Petch over a muted condition. Locked tiles have a dashed line rim, no fill, 60% opacity and a muted name. In the player plate the current title is a holo parallelogram tag with void text; with none it is an 8% white tag with muted text ("ยังไม่มีฉายา").

### Level-Up Window
A modal in the frame (16px radius) with the header "ประกาศจากระบบ", "LEVEL UP!", "Lv. a → Lv. b", the 150px crest, a rank-change line if the rank changed, a muted note and a full-width big holo button. Behind it, gold rays turn every 18s. It enters from 0.7 scale and 3× brightness over 0.7s, with a warm full-screen flash, 28 sparks bursting outward, a three-note rising beep and a vibration. Sparks and the flash are skipped under reduced motion.

### Buttons
- **Default:** 8px radius, 44px minimum, a white 8% to 2% gradient, 14% white rim, 10% top highlight, ink text. Hover turns the rim cyan.
- **Primary / Hot:** chamfered (10px cut, 6px small), no border, the holo with a white light band, void text at 700. The start button is 60px with a 1.3rem label, and the big primary in a mission plate runs the light band across every 3s. Hover brightens to 1.08.
- **Small** 36px, **big** 54px, `wide` full width. **Danger:** no fill, bad-colour text and rim. **Ghost:** transparent. **Link** (hero only): borderless, underlined, cyan. **Disabled:** 40% opacity.
- **Solid primary:** on the rest timer, the HIIT overlay and the onboarding footer the primary is solid cyan with void text, still chamfered.
- **Press:** scales to 0.97 over 0.12s. Focus is a 2px cyan outline offset by 2px, everywhere.

### Chips
- **Style:** 6px radius, 0.86rem 600, sunk fill and muted text. `acc` is accent-soft with ink text and `hot` is hot-soft with magenta text. Good, warn and bad recolour only the text.
- **State:** button chips are 34px tall and fill cyan with void text when pressed.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 12px.
- **Background:** card-top to card-bottom inside a 1px hairline frame of cyan 35%, white 6% and magenta 30%.
- **Shadow Strategy:** the card drop.
- **Internal Padding:** 18px (tips 16px, tools 14px).
- **Tools:** three tiles, each a 48px item icon over a Chakra Petch name and a muted caption.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** full width, field fill, field-line rim, 8px radius, 11px × 13px padding, 46px minimum height, and 16px text so iOS never zooms. Big-number inputs use Chakra Petch 600 at 1.25rem.
- **Focus:** the rim turns cyan with a 3px cyan 18% ring, plus the 2px cyan outline.
- **Segments and options:** segments are 40px pills that fill cyan when checked. Option cards have a 1.5px line rim and turn cyan-rimmed on accent-soft when checked.

### Navigation
- **Phone:** a floating bar, 16px radius, with a near-opaque card fill, hairline rim and the nav drop. Each of five columns is a 30px item icon over a 0.8rem muted label. Inactive icons are 75% greyscale at 75% brightness. The active one returns to full colour, gains a cyan glow and lifts 2px at 1.08 scale, and its label turns white 600. The centre "ฝึก" item is a 58px holo diamond raised 28px above the bar with the dumbbell item inside.
- **Desktop (880px and up):** pill tabs in the top bar. The active tab is surface with a deep drop.
- **Top bar:** sticky, 56px, top glass (blur 16px, saturate 1.4) with a 6% white hairline underneath.

### Live counters
The rep stepper is a 64px / 1fr / 64px grid with a 3.2rem numeral. Set dots are 40px rounded squares: dashed while pending, green-rimmed when done, cyan for the current set. The rest timer is a void card (22px radius) with a 4.2rem countdown and a solid cyan primary. The HIIT overlay is full-screen void with a clock up to 8rem, and its phase label turns cyan during work. While you rest away from the player, a rest bar (12px radius, dark gradient, hairline rim) floats above the nav with an ink countdown and a muted label.

### Account and sync card
The Profile account card (sign-up, log-in, sync status, pairing) is an ordinary card built from the same parts: a chip toggle, fields, a full-width primary button, and a row of small buttons ending in danger. Forms, chips, the Google button, the "or" divider and notes keep a 460px maximum line length on wide screens. The Google sign-in button must keep Google's own white button (#FFF fill, #1F1F1F text, #747775 rim) as a vendor requirement. The pairing QR sits on white (180px, 12px radius) so it scans. Recovery and pair codes are monospace, on surface with a 10px radius, and select-all. The pair code is 2rem with 0.12em tracking.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** put the game in mission plates: 18px chamfers at top-left and bottom-right, the iridescent frame, the lit violet interior and the light sweep. Put information and tools in 12px rounded cards with the hairline frame.
- **Do** keep the holo (#3DE0FF → #8B7CFF → #FF4FD8 → #FFC857) for the wordmark's "Fit", primary actions, the train diamond, the crest ring, the earned title tag and completed days, with #05060B text on it.
- **Do** give every quest exactly one colour (training #FFC857, recovery #2DD4BF, water #3DE0FF, protein #FF8A3D, steps #3EE6A0, sleep #8B7CFF) for its row tint, item frame, bar and done rim.
- **Do** draw quests, tools and nav items as illustrated item icons from the one registry, framed in an octagon when they belong to a quest.
- **Do** show standing through the rank crest: rank colour on its rim, letter and aura, the holo ring outside, using the ladder E #A3B3CE, D #4ADE80, C #38BDF8, B #A78BFA, A #FB923C, S #FACC15.
- **Do** let quests be logged where they are listed, with 34px inline controls and 16px input text.
- **Do** set headings, numerals and HUD labels in Chakra Petch with tabular figures for numbers, and every sentence in Sarabun.
- **Do** keep muted text at #A9B1CC or lighter (8.1:1 or better on every plate, card and modal), body at 16.5px and Thai small text at 0.86rem or larger.
- **Do** keep touch targets at 44px or more (34-36px for compact chips, small buttons and inline quest controls) and form inputs at 16px text.
- **Do** keep all motion behind `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`; the motes draw one still frame and pause when the page is hidden.

### Don't:
- **Don't** reintroduce a light theme or theme-dependent tokens. The world is a single dark scheme.
- **Don't** chamfer ordinary information cards or round off a mission plate. The cut corner marks the game layer.
- **Don't** clip the holo into any text other than the wordmark's "Fit" and earned title names.
- **Don't** let two quests on the same day share a colour, or use magenta as a quest colour.
- **Don't** glow body text, list rows or secondary controls.
- **Don't** use line icons where an item icon exists for the thing.
- **Don't** copy names, terms or text from Solo Leveling or any other IP. The labels are MewFit's own Thai ("สถานะผู้เล่น", "ภารกิจวันนี้", "ประกาศภารกิจ", "ประกาศจากระบบ").
- **Don't** set paragraphs in Chakra Petch or put Thai below 0.86rem outside the nav labels.
- **Don't** use the retired orange-red mark or flatten the kettlebell-cat logo to one hue.

## Readability (added 2026-10-29, updated for the Forge skin)
The owner found earlier text hard to read, so these rules hold. Body text is Sarabun, a looped Thai face, at 16.5px with 1.7 line height. Muted text is #A9B1CC, which measures 9.5:1 on void, 8.3:1 on the plate interior, 8.2:1 on cards and 8.1:1 in modals. Ink #EEF2FF is 15.4:1 or better everywhere. Every colour used as text clears 5.2:1 on the darkest-lit surfaces: gold 11.2:1, cyan 10.9:1, green 10.7:1, teal 9.3:1, orange 7.3:1, magenta 6.0:1, bad 5.7:1 and violet 5.3:1. Void text on the holo is 6.2:1 on its violet stop and 7.1:1 or better on the others. Thai small text never drops below 0.86rem except the nav labels (0.8rem). The Latin HUD labels are 0.8rem and "+n XP" is 0.84rem. The coach's line in the hero sits on a 4% white wash with an 8% rim, in ink. During a workout (`body.playing`) the floating nav is hidden so the rep stepper and log button stay clear.
