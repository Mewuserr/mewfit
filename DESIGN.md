# Design System: MewFit

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Neon Arcade"**

MewFit plays like an arcade RPG. The page is a deep violet-black night with an aurora behind it: a violet bloom in the top-left corner, a faint cyan one at the top right, and a pink one rising from the bottom right. Every panel is a pane of frosted glass that the aurora glows through. The brand is one four-stop gradient, cyan through indigo and orchid to pink. It is the logo, the "Fit" of the wordmark, the primary action and the frame of the cards that carry the game. Five arcade colours (cyan, violet, pink, green, amber) give each daily quest and each stat its own colour, so the screen reads at a glance. Amber also serves as gold, the colour of the bonus XP and LEVEL UP. Your standing is a rank from E to S, shown as a round medal whose gradient ring is your XP progress to the next level. The spirit is the daily-quest status screens of hunter-ranking manhwa, but none of that IP's names or text are used. Every label is MewFit's own Thai.

The Today view opens on two gradient-framed cards. The status card holds the rank medal, the player name, level and rank name, a title pill, the XP caption and the five stats (STR, AGI, VIT, END, INT). Below it, the daily-quest card holds a gradient progress bar and count, the coach's line, the strip of today's moves, the quest list, the bonus strip and the start button. Each quest is logged in place: water with − and +1, steps and sleep with number inputs, protein with a link to the Eat view. Everything else (food, plan, body tracking, knowledge) sits in plain glass cards lower down. Live numbers (reps, rest, interval clock) are huge tabular numerals in Chakra Petch.

The app is dark only. `color-scheme` is `dark` and the page `theme-color` is night. The layout is built for phones first (390px design width, safe down to 360px), with 16px gutters, a 14px rhythm between blocks, and touch targets of at least 44px. Compact chips and inline quest controls are 34px.

**Identity asset.** The logo and every app icon (`icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`, `icon-maskable-512.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, and `icon.svg`, which wraps the PNG in a 15/64-radius clip) show a kettlebell whose handle forms a cat's ears, with eyes, nose and whiskers cut out as negative space. The shape was first generated with ChatGPT image generation on 2026-10-29 at the owner's request, replacing a leaping-cat mark the owner rejected as too close to Puma. That first mark was off-white on orange-red. For the neon arcade skin the same shape was rebuilt locally from the ChatGPT mark's outline and re-coloured: the kettlebell-cat is filled with the cyan-violet-pink brand gradient, sits on a deep indigo field and has a soft glow. It was then exported locally to every size. The logo shows as the 34px mark in the top bar and the 72px mark in the onboarding hero, both with a 10px radius. The wordmark "Mew**Fit**" is set in Chakra Petch, with "Fit" filled with the brand gradient.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Aurora night (violet, cyan and pink blooms on night) behind frosted-glass cards.
- One brand gradient (cyan, indigo, orchid, pink) for the logo, the wordmark's "Fit", primary actions, the frames of the game cards and modals, and the medal ring.
- Five arcade colours, one per quest and stat, each with its own tinted icon tile. Gold (amber) for the bonus XP and LEVEL UP.
- The rank is a round medal whose ring is the XP progress.
- Chakra Petch sets headings, numerals and HUD labels. Sarabun, a looped Thai face, carries every sentence.
- Soft, rounded forms: 20px cards, 12-16px controls, pills and circles.
- Animated stick-figure demos in ink on dark glass, with pink props.

## Colors

A violet-black night lit by an aurora, with one brand gradient and five arcade colours that each own a quest and a stat.

### Primary
- **Brand Gradient** (arcade-cyan to grad-indigo at 45%, grad-orchid at 70%, arcade-pink at 100%, at 120 degrees): the brand. It fills primary and hot buttons, the raised nav button, the move-number circles in the move strip, the quest progress bar, the earned title pill, the completed bonus strip and completed week days. It is the 1px border of the status card, the quest card and every modal, and the ring of the rank medal. It is clipped into text for exactly two things: "Fit" in the wordmark and earned title names.
- **Arcade Ink** (arcade-ink): text and icons on the gradient and on solid arcade colours (6.4:1 on the indigo stop, 7.2:1 or better on the others). The title pill uses a near-identical #1A0B33.
- **Go Lavender** (go-lavender): the solid primary used where the gradient is not applied: primary buttons on the rest timer, the HIIT overlay and the onboarding footer and the active HIIT phase label. It takes arcade-ink text.

### Secondary: the arcade five
Each colour owns one quest and one stat. It colours that row's icon tile (18% tint, icon in full colour), progress bar and +XP amount, and the stat's key, cell tint and bar.
- **Arcade Cyan** (arcade-cyan): the water quest and AGI. It also marks the active nav icon, today in the week strip, the tool icons and the hero link.
- **Arcade Violet** (arcade-violet): the sleep quest and INT. It is also `--accent`, the interface's selection colour: links, focus rings, the caret, text selection, selected chips, checked segments and options, default progress bars, `summary` toggles, the current set dot and the onboarding progress.
- **Arcade Pink** (arcade-pink): the protein quest and STR. It is also `--hot`: figure props, the numbered onboarding feature tiles, `hot` chips and the sleep dot on the timeline.
- **Arcade Green** (arcade-green): the steps quest, the recovery quest on rest days, and VIT. It is also `--good`: done sets, food dots, water glasses and verdicts.
- **Arcade Amber** (arcade-amber): the main training quest and END. As `--gold` it is the bonus "+100 XP" and the "LEVEL UP!" headline with its gold glow. As `--warn` it marks over-target bars and caution lines.

### Tertiary
- **Violet Soft** (violet-soft) / **Pink Soft** (pink-soft): tinted fills. Violet soft backs training days, selected options, banners, OK notes, list hover, tool icon tiles and the top of the medal's inner disc. Pink soft backs `hot` chips, notes and the onboarding feature tiles.
- **Aurora Violet / Aurora Pink**: the background blooms only (at 38% and 22%). The cyan bloom is arcade-cyan at 18%.

### Rank ladder
- **E** (rank-e, steel), **D** (rank-d, green), **C** (rank-c, sky), **B** (rank-b, violet), **A** (rank-a, orange), **S** (rank-s, gold). The current rank's colour is passed to the medal as `--rk`. It colours only the medal letter and its glow.

### Neutral
- **Night** (night): the page ground under the aurora, the safe-area strip, the nav cut-out ring and `theme-color`.
- **Glass** (glass) with **Glass Line** (glass-line): the fill and 1px rim of every plain card, tip, tool tile and the hero, over a 16px backdrop blur. Glass line also rims buttons, inputs, the nav bar, the toast, the rest bar and the move-strip chips, and rules the KPI row.
- **Frame Top / Frame Bottom**: the opaque fill inside the gradient border of the status card, quest card and modals, so the gradient reads as a clean edge.
- **Top Glass** (top-glass) and **Nav Glass** (nav-glass): the sticky top bar and the floating nav. **Well** (well): every input field and the figure panel.
- **Wash** (wash) and **Track** (track): white tints for quiet surfaces. Quest rows and the bonus strip use 4%, title tiles, week days and the coach's line 5%, default buttons and move chips 6%, and stat bars 7%. Track (8%) holds every progress track and the unearned title pill.
- **Surface** (surface): the base panel colour under the skin, still used by desktop tabs, segments, option cards and the recovery code. **Sunk** (sunk): chips, target tiles and bar tracks. **Toast** (toast): the toast fill.
- **Ink** (ink): text and figure strokes. **White** (white): the active nav label and text on rope. **Muted** (muted): secondary text, nav labels, captions and counts. **Line** (line): hairlines between list rows and table cells.
- **Rope** (rope), **Rope Line** (rope-line), **Rope Track** (rope-track): the rest timer card and the full-screen HIIT overlay, their button outlines and figure props, and the HIIT progress track. **Figure Prop** (figure-prop): stick-figure props and ground line. The hero figure uses rope line instead.

### Semantic
- **Good** is arcade-green and **Warn** is arcade-amber (see above). **Bad** (bad): the danger button and the "ความเชื่อ:" myth prefix.

### Named Rules
**The Gradient Is The Brand Rule.** The four-stop gradient means MewFit itself or the main thing to do next: the logo, the wordmark, the primary action, the game cards' frames and the medal ring. It is not a decoration for ordinary cards, lists or body text.

**The One Colour Per Quest Rule.** Each quest and stat owns one arcade colour, and every mark for it (icon tile, bar, +XP, stat key, done tint) uses that colour. Colour identifies the quest. Done state is also shown by the check icon and the muted +XP, never by colour alone.

**The Rank Lights The Letter Rule.** The rank colour appears only on the medal letter and its glow. The ring is always the brand gradient, because it shows XP, not rank.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Chakra Petch (with Kanit, Leelawadee UI, Noto Sans Thai, system-ui), loaded at 500-700.
**Body Font:** Sarabun (with Leelawadee UI, Noto Sans Thai Looped, Noto Sans Thai, system-ui). It was chosen in a readability pass after the owner said the text was hard to read.
**Numeral Font:** Chakra Petch with tabular figures.
**Data Font:** the system monospace stack, used only in the backup/restore JSON textarea (0.8rem) and the recovery code (1.25rem, 0.06em tracking).

**Character:** Chakra Petch is squared and technical, and covers Thai, so headings, numbers and HUD labels read like an arcade interface. Sarabun's looped Thai forms keep the coaching copy calm and easy to read.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (700, clamp(2rem, 8.5vw, 2.8rem), 1.12): the session name in the training-day hero.
- **Headline** (700, clamp(1.8rem, 7.4vw, 2.5rem), 1.2): the page `h1`. Onboarding uses clamp(1.9rem, 7vw, 2.6rem) with -0.02em tracking.
- **Card Header** (700, 1.15rem): the title row of the status, quest and titles cards, in ink. A muted Sarabun 0.9rem sub-label (date and time left, titles earned) sits at the right of the same row.
- **Title** (700, 1.25rem): card `h2`. Player move names scale to clamp(1.4rem, 5vw, 1.9rem) (Title Player), and the HIIT move name to clamp(1.4rem, 5vw, 2.2rem) (Title HIIT). **Title Small** (600, 1.05rem): `h3`, option titles and buttons. **Status Name** (700, 1.5rem, 1.15): the player name.
- **Medal Letter** (700, 2.7rem, 3.4rem on the level-up medal): the rank letter. **Level Up** (700, 2.7rem, 0.05em, gold with glow).
- **Body** (400, 16.5px, 1.7): all reading text, capped at 52-62ch. **Quest Text** is body at 600 with 1.35 line height. Small text is 0.93rem.
- **Label** (Sarabun 500-600, 0.86-0.92rem): chips, small buttons, tile captions, table heads and field labels. **Nav Label** (0.8rem) is the one Thai exception below 0.86rem. **HUD Label** (Chakra Petch 0.86rem): the XP caption, quest counts and +XP. **Stat Key** (0.72rem, 0.08em) is the smallest type in the system. It holds only the three-letter Latin stat codes, never Thai. Thai has no case and nothing is uppercased by CSS.
- **Numerals** (Chakra Petch, tabular): metrics at 1.05rem 600, stat values at 1.3rem 700, the level number at 1.35rem, and KPIs as a ruled row at 2.1rem. The rest bar countdown is 1.5rem. Live numerals step up by scale: the rep stepper is 3.2rem, the rest timer 4.2rem and the HIIT clock clamp(4rem, 20vw, 8rem). Chakra Petch tops out at 700, so the 800 weights declared in the CSS render at 700.

### Named Rules
**The Two Voices Rule.** Chakra Petch is the system (headings, numbers, HUD labels, buttons). Sarabun is the coach (sentences and quest text). Never set a paragraph in Chakra Petch.

**The Live Number Rule.** The number the user is acting on right now is the largest thing on screen (3.2rem or more), tabular and in ink. Colour isn't used to make it louder.

**The Two Gradient Words Rule.** Gradient-clipped text is used for exactly two things: "Fit" in the wordmark and earned title names. Everything else that reads is set in solid colour.

## Layout

The page is one centred column, 1000px wide at most, with 16px gutters that respect `env(safe-area-inset-*)`. Each view is a vertical stack with a 14px gap. Game cards have 18px padding and a 14px inner gap. Plain cards stack singly on phones and form 2- or 3-up grids from 740px. KPI stats auto-fit at a 130px minimum, form fields at 140px, and titles at 150px.

The status card puts the 112px medal beside the identity column (18px gap). The five stats sit in a fixed 5-column grid with a 6px gap. On the Progress view they become rows (48px key / bar / value) with a muted explanation under each. Quest rows are a 44px / 1fr / auto grid with a 12px gap and 10px × 12px padding, stacked 10px apart. The right column holds the +XP amount over the row's control.

Navigation is a floating bar fixed 10px above the bottom safe area. It sits 12px from the sides, is at most 520px wide and has five columns. Main content reserves 116px of bottom padding for it. The bar is hidden during a workout and during onboarding. At 880px it gives way to pill tabs in the top bar. On wide screens the workout player puts the figure in a sticky left column.

Breakpoints: 420px (tool grid), 640px (hero and option grids), 740px (card grids), 760px (player split), 880px (desktop nav).

## Elevation & Depth

Depth comes from glass over light. The aurora lights the field, and each card is a frosted pane (16px blur, 1.3 saturation) that lets the colour through and sits on a soft, deep drop shadow. The game cards and modals are more solid (92% fill) and are edged in the brand gradient instead of the glass rim. Coloured light is kept for things that matter: the gradient buttons and the raised nav button cast a violet glow, the medal floats on a violet halo, its letter glows in the rank colour, a completed quest's icon tile glows in its quest colour, the stat bars glow faintly, and LEVEL UP glows gold. The modal scrim blurs (4px) instead of casting a shadow.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Glass drop** (`box-shadow: 0 18px 40px -24px rgba(0,0,0,.9)`): every card, game card, tip, tool tile, modal and hero, and the active desktop tab.
- **Gradient lift** (`box-shadow: 0 8px 22px -10px rgba(192,132,252,.9), inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,.45)`): gradient primary and hot buttons.
- **Nav drop** (`box-shadow: 0 16px 40px -16px rgba(0,0,0,.9)`): the floating nav bar. The raised button adds `0 0 0 5px var(--bg), 0 10px 26px -6px rgba(192,132,252,.9)`, where the night ring cuts it out of the bar.
- **Medal halo** (`filter: drop-shadow(0 6px 18px rgba(168,85,247,.45))`, letter `text-shadow: 0 0 18px` of the rank colour at 70%): the rank medal.
- **Quest glow** (`box-shadow: 0 0 18px -4px var(--qc)`): a completed quest's icon tile. Stat bars use `0 0 12px -2px var(--sc)`.
- **Reward glow** (`text-shadow: 0 0 22px rgba(251,191,36,.65)`): the LEVEL UP headline.
- **Rest drop** (`box-shadow: 0 14px 30px -14px rgba(0,0,0,.5)`): the floating rest bar.

### Named Rules
**The Light Means Live Rule.** Coloured light appears only on the primary action, the medal, a completed quest, a stat bar or LEVEL UP. Plain text, list rows and secondary controls never glow.

## Shapes

Soft and rounded. Cards, game cards, tips, tool tiles, the hero and modals use a 20px radius, as do the rest bar and the hero figure panel. Buttons are 14px (small 11px, big 16px, inline quest controls 10px). Inputs are 12px. Quest rows are 16px and their icon tiles 13px. Stat cells are 12px, and title tiles and the bonus strip 14px. The floating nav is 22px. Chips, segments, the move strip, top tabs, progress tracks and the title pill are full pills. Circles are used for the rank medal, the raised nav button, move numbers and legend dots. The medal is a ring 7px thick around an inner disc lit from the upper middle (violet soft fading to sunk). The logo tile is 10px.

Some inner controls inherited from earlier builds keep their own radii: the rest timer card 22px, option cards 18px, banners and figure boxes 16px, week days, notes, target tiles and the toast 14px, the recovery code 10px, and the water glasses 2px. They sit within the 10-22px range of the world.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** put new panels in a glass card: glass fill, 16px backdrop blur, 1px glass-line rim, 20px radius and the glass drop. Use the gradient-bordered game card only for the status card, the quest card and modals.
- **Do** keep the brand gradient (#22D3EE → #818CF8 → #C084FC → #F472B6) for the logo, the wordmark's "Fit", primary actions, the game-card frames, the medal ring, quest progress and earned titles, with #14082E text on it.
- **Do** give every quest and stat exactly one arcade colour (cyan #22D3EE, violet #A78BFA, pink #F472B6, green #34D399, amber #FBBF24) and use it for its icon tile, bar, +XP and stat key.
- **Do** show standing only through the rank medal: the letter in the rank colour, the ring as XP progress, using the ladder E #A3B3CE, D #4ADE80, C #38BDF8, B #A78BFA, A #FB923C, S #FACC15.
- **Do** let quests be logged where they are listed, with 34px inline controls and 16px input text.
- **Do** set headings, numerals and HUD labels in Chakra Petch with tabular figures for numbers, and every sentence in Sarabun.
- **Do** keep muted text at #B8AED6 or lighter (7.5:1 or better on glass over the brightest bloom), body at 16.5px, small text at 0.86rem or larger, and the 0.72rem stat key for Latin codes only.
- **Do** keep touch targets at 44px or more (34-36px for compact chips, small buttons and inline quest controls) and form inputs at 16px text.
- **Do** keep all motion behind `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`. The game-card rise is the one authored entrance.

### Don't:
- **Don't** reintroduce a light theme or theme-dependent tokens. The world is a single dark scheme.
- **Don't** bring back the retired system-window skin: corner brackets, cyan rims and cyan window glows.
- **Don't** clip the gradient into any text other than the wordmark's "Fit" and earned title names.
- **Don't** let two quests on the same day share a colour, or use a quest colour for anything but its own quest and stat.
- **Don't** glow plain text, list rows or secondary controls. Light marks the primary action, the medal, completed quests, stat bars and LEVEL UP.
- **Don't** copy names, terms or text from Solo Leveling or any other IP. The labels are MewFit's own Thai ("สถานะผู้เล่น", "เควสประจำวัน", "แจ้งเตือนจากระบบ").
- **Don't** set paragraphs in Chakra Petch or put Thai in the 0.72rem stat-key size.
- **Don't** use the retired orange-red mark or flatten the kettlebell-cat logo to one hue. It carries the brand gradient on deep indigo.

## Readability (added 2026-10-29, updated for the arcade skin)
The owner found earlier text hard to read, so these rules hold. Body text is Sarabun, a looped Thai face, at 16.5px with 1.7 line height. Muted text is #B8AED6, which measures 9.5:1 on the night field, 8.8:1 on glass, 8.6:1 on surface, and 7.5:1 on glass over the brightest point of the violet bloom. Ink #F3EEFF is 13.8:1 or better everywhere. Every arcade colour used as text (+XP, stat keys) is at least 5.7:1 on glass, and arcade-ink is at least 6.4:1 on every stop of the gradient. Small text never drops below 0.86rem except the Latin stat codes (0.72rem) and the nav labels (0.8rem). The coach's line in the hero sits on a 5% white wash in ink at weight 400. During a workout (`body.playing`) the floating nav is hidden so the rep stepper and log button stay clear.
